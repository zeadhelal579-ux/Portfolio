Who I am

I'm a second-year Computer Science student at the Faculty of Computers and Information, Suez University, and a coach and organizer at Anomaly Community, where I lead structured technical training for members learning web development.

My work spans four areas: frontend interfaces, backend systems, networking, and hardware/embedded projects — I like understanding a system end to end, not just one layer of it. I've finished learning React, I'm now learning Next.js, and I'm working on full-stack projects.

I take part in hackathons and competitions, and I'm passionate about technology, especially web development and AI.
